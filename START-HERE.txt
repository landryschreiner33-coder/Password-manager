OPEN STILLWATER ON YOUR MAC

1. Double-click Stillwater.xcodeproj inside this folder.
2. In Xcode select the Stillwater target, then Signing & Capabilities.
3. Choose your Personal Team with Automatically manage signing checked.
4. Select the same team for StillwaterTests if Xcode asks.
5. Select Stillwater / My Mac at the top, then press Command-R.

The app icon is already installed. All permissions files are included.
Keep this whole project folder together: App and Core must stay next to
Stillwater.xcodeproj. Do not move the xcodeproj out on its own.

INSTALL THE APP
After a successful build, open Products in Xcode's left sidebar.
Right-click Stillwater.app and choose Show in Finder.
Quit Stillwater, copy the built app to Applications, then open that copy.
You can close Xcode. Right-click the running Dock icon and choose
Options > Keep in Dock.

This project does not include your personal Apple signing certificate.
You select your team locally in Xcode. Your vault is stored separately
from this project folder; do not delete or reset the vault to fix a build.
